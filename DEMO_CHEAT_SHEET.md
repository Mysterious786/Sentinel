### **Browser** (http://localhost:3000)

- Zoom: 110% for readability
- Hide bookmarks bar

### **Recording Software**
- **macOS**: QuickTime (Cmd+Space → QuickTime → File → New Screen Recording)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps

### **Audio**
- Speak 20% slower than normal
- Clear pronunciation
- Pause after key points
- Build excitement throughout

## ⚠️ **COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID**

### **Technical Issues**
- Don't click simulate too fast (wait for response)
- Don't scroll too quickly (let viewers read)
- Don't skip over key metrics

### **Narration Issues**  
- Don't rush through numbers
- Don't forget to explain "why this matters"
- Don't use too much technical jargon

### **Visual Issues**
- Don't cover important text with cursor
- Don't use unclear mouse movements
- Don't record with notifications on
